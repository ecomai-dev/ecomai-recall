# EcomAI Recall™

Save your AI chats in one click. Switch between ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and others without losing your context.

### 🔄 What It Does
- Save any AI conversation as a `.txt` file
- Load it into another AI and continue your chat
- Cross-AI memory – seamless conversation handoff
- Offline, secure, RAM-friendly

### 🌍 Why It Matters
Built for researchers, developers, and creators who use multiple AI tools. Never copy-paste again. No RAM drain. Just pure productivity.

### 📦 How to Use
1. Click “Save Memory” to download your conversation
2. Use “Load Memory” to upload and continue in any AI chat
3. Works with ChatGPT, Claude, Grok (and more coming)
